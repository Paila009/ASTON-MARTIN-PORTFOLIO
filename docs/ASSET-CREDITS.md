## Design reference research

[Lando Norris](https://landonorris.com/) was inspected on 18 October 2026 as requested. Its live page provides an immersive sticky opening, large split typography, a next-race micro-panel, contrasting on-track / off-track storytelling, and an interactive helmet collection, supported by Rive/canvas elements. These principles inform an original Aston Martin composition: the car as the focal point, green material lighting, fine engineering labels and concise editorial routes. No Lando Norris artwork, source code, branded assets or copy is included in this project.

[Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/) informed the 2026 subject matter, car/driver imagery, engineering vocabulary and the relationship between the technology campus and race team. See the separate data-source notes for factual verification.
